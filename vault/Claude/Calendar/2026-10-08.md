## Deep research for weapons, sword skills and animations

Henry got the Max plan and asked for end-to-end parallel research to plan the base of the game: fix all animations, design sword skills, and add melee weapon classes (dagger, longsword, axe and more; no ranged), inspired by SAO Echoes of Aincrad and souls games.

Five research agents ran in parallel:
- [[Echoes of Aincrad and SAO action games (2026-10-08)]]
- [[Souls melee weapon class design (2026-10-08)]]
- [[Sword Skill system design (2026-10-08)]]
- [[Roblox combat animation pipeline (2026-10-08)]]
- [[Weapon class progression and switching (2026-10-08)]]

Reddit was blocked in all five. Forum evidence came from Steam, GameFAQs, official forums and the DevForum. One Echoes fan site (echoesofaincrad.org) was found to list weapons the game doesn't have, so its weapon page was excluded.

The draft is in [[Weapons and sword skills plan]]. Three decisions are open: the launch roster, whether a roll can cancel skill post-motion, and how to polish animations.

No game or code changes today.
