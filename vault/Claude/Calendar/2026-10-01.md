## Parry window fix

Henry found parries too hard to hit.

Changed in the live place:
- `CombatConfig`
  - ParryWindow 0.2→0.3.
  - New ParrySpamLockout 0.4, ParryWindowSpammed 0.07 and MaxParryRewind 0.15.
  - New attribute name BlockParryWindow.
- `CombatService`
  - `onAction("Block", true, clientServerTime)`: the block start time is the client's press time, clamped to at most 0.15 s before the server received it.
  - Re-raising block within 0.4 s of releasing it gets the 0.07 s window.
  - New `actor.lastBlockRelease`.
  - `setState` clears BlockParryWindow.
- `DamageService`: the parry check reads the per-block BlockParryWindow attribute (falls back to ParryWindow).
- `WeaponController`: sends `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` with Block true.
- Docs.Combat defense section and the README changelog.

Verified in a Studio playtest:
- Server-side checks:
  - A fresh block gets the 0.3 window.
  - Re-raising after 0.2 s gets 0.07.
  - Raising after a 0.6 s gap gets 0.3 again.
  - A faked press time 2 s in the past is clamped to 0.15 s.
  - Attributes clear on release.
- Against the Sparring Knight:
  - Block raised 0.25 s before the hit parries.
  - Block raised 0.35 s before the hit is a normal block with chip damage.

## Riposte finisher and class defaults

Plan item #2. Attacking an enemy whose posture or guard is broken plays a riposte instead of a normal light.

Changed in the live place:
- New `Combat.ClassDefaults` module. `WeaponRegistry` merges it into each weapon definition for any field the weapon leaves nil, working on a `table.clone` copy because required module tables can be frozen. Every sword (StarterSword, TestSword) gets the riposte this way, and future weapon classes add their own entry.
- `Types`: new `RiposteDefinition` (Animation, DamageMultiplier, Range, Weight, Sound) and an optional `Riposte` field on WeaponDefinition.
- `CombatConfig`: RiposteMarkColor and a StaggerKind attribute name.
- `PoiseService`: `Stagger` tags the model with StaggerKind (Stagger, GuardBreak, ParryRecoil, Riposted) and clears it when the stagger ends.
- `CombatService`: new "Riposte" attack. The server checks that the target is alive, staggered with kind Stagger or GuardBreak, in range, and damageable. It then holds the target in a "Riposted" stagger, gives the attacker i-frames for the action, and resolves one hit at 2.5x damage with no poise damage. Normal hit reports are ignored during a riposte.
- `Specs`: new Riposte animation (thrust, hold, rip out, settle) with a new RipOut marker. AnimSaves rebuilt.
- `BaseWeapon`: preloads the riposte animation, and fires any extra marker that has a callback.
- `WeaponController`: finds a riposte target (lock-on target first, else the nearest open enemy in front), dashes in, and plays the riposte with spark, red flash, hit stop, shake and impact frame. Open enemies get a red highlight marker.
- Docs.Combat riposte section and the README changelog.

Verified in a Studio playtest:
- Server path: Riposted stagger, about 1.04 s of i-frames, 45 damage (12 x 2.5 x 1.5 crit), and the marker turns on and off correctly.
- Full client click path against the Sparring Knight: knight HP 193 to 148, back to Idle at about 1.06 s, no console errors.

Git: committed as f567dec, "Riposte finisher on staggered or guard-broken enemies + class defaults". Henry needs to push. The repo matched the live place 62/62 before committing.

## Contextual attacks

Plan item #3. Left click does a contextual attack instead of Light1 when the situation matches. Priority is riposte, then guard counter, jump, roll, running, then a normal light. It never interrupts a light combo, and holding the button never turns these into a heavy.

Research (subagent pass, Reddit blocked, so GameFAQs and Steam were used): Elden Ring stance damage on a longsword is running R2 2x an R1, jump R2 5x, and guard counter 7.5x (aoeah mirror of a community sheet, so treat the ratios as approximate). Players call ER jump attacks overtuned (GameFAQs, Steam). ER jump attacks from a walk or sprint have super armor until landing (mmopixel). One player claims the guard counter window is about 1 s (Steam). DS3 allows an attack right after a roll or backstep (Fextralife DS3). Exact motion values and windows were not found, so our numbers are judgment calls.

Changed in the live place:
- `ClassDefaults.Sword`, scaled from Light1 (1.0x damage, 12 poise):
  - RunningAttack 1.3x damage, 24 poise, 16 stamina, Lunge 2.2.
  - JumpAttack 1.5x damage, 30 poise, 18 stamina, HyperArmor, Lunge 1, Plunge 55.
  - RollAttack 1.1x damage, 14 poise, 12 stamina, Lunge 1.6.
  - GuardCounter 1.6x damage, 36 poise, 16 stamina, HyperArmor, Lunge 1.2.
- `Types`: optional RunningAttack, JumpAttack, RollAttack and GuardCounter on WeaponDefinition, plus `Plunge` on AttackDefinition.
- `CombatConfig`: RunningAttackMinSpeed 20, RollAttackWindow 0.3, GuardCounterWindow 0.6, JumpAttackGrace 0.25, and a GuardCounterUntil attribute.
- `WeaponRegistry`: validates the new animations. `BaseWeapon` preloads them. `AnimationLibrary.Published` lists them, along with Riposte, for upload.
- `DamageService`: a parry or a non-guard-break block sets GuardCounterUntil = now + 0.6 on the blocker.
- `CombatService`: a new ("Context", name) attack. Checks per attack:
  - Running: the Sprinting attribute or the speed.
  - Jump: a server ground raycast within the grace.
  - Roll: during the roll or within the window after it.
  - Guard: GuardCounterUntil hasn't expired, and using the counter clears it.
  - The server sees positions about 0.2 s late (measured), so a jump attack waits up to the grace for the server to see the jump. Humanoid.FloorMaterial doesn't work on the server for players (tested).
- `WeaponController`: pickContextAttack and startContext, plunge on the Lunge marker, and a camera punch for heavy contextual attacks. There's also a Studio-only `Players.<you>.CombatTestInput` BindableEvent for exact-timing tests.
- `Specs`: RunningAttack, JumpAttack, RollAttack and GuardCounter. AnimSaves rebuilt (21).
- Docs.Combat CONTEXTUAL ATTACKS and TESTING sections, and the README changelog.

Verified in a Studio playtest against the training dummy, using CombatTestInput for timing:
- Roll attack works out of a backstep, and also 0.15 s after it, for 13.2 damage. Attacking 0.55 s after the backstep gives a normal Light1.
- Jump attack pressed 0.1 s and 0.22 s into a jump is accepted and hits for 18. Pressed 0.45 s in (landing), it gives Light1.
- The running attack is accepted at 26 studs/s. Its lunge peaks at 34 studs/s, then slows to attack speed.
- Guard counter: after a real DamageService block (BlockImpact), attacking gives GuardCounter for 19.2 damage, and the attribute clears after use.
- The server rejects all four when the situation is faked from the client, and ignores unknown names.
- No regressions: the Light1-3 combo and heavy hold still work. No console errors.

Not tuned yet: the running attack drops to attack speed right after its lunge. A longer carry may feel better.

## Directional rolls while locked on

Plan item #4. The source is the Elden Ring note in [[Combat and movement research (2026-09)]]: directional rolls only play when locked on. Snapping diagonals to the nearest direction is a judgment call.

Changed in the live place (Studio id changed to f5d61401-6dd6-41af-9ae8-036da9f15c65 after Henry reopened it):
- `WeaponController.startRoll`: while locked on with move input, `lockedRollAnimation` compares the input with the direction to the target. The larger component picks Roll, RollBack, RollLeft or RollRight. Facing stays on the target (SetFacing nil) and the dash follows the exact input. Unlocked rolls and the backstep are unchanged. The new animations are preloaded and stopped in cancelAll.
- `CombatService`: ("Roll", direction, animation) takes the animation from a whitelist (ROLL_ANIMATIONS) and uses its markers for i-frames and timings. Anything else falls back to Roll.
- `Specs`: RollBack mirrors the forward roll (+X rotation). RollLeft and RollRight rotate about Z (+Z tips the head left). The new helpers are `sideRollPose` and `sideRollSpec`. Keyframes are no more than 90 degrees apart so the rotation interpolates the right way. Same markers as Roll (IFrame 0.05 to 0.35, CancelOpen 0.5, length 0.62). AnimSaves rebuilt (24).
- `AnimationLibrary.Published` lists the three new animations.
- Docs.Combat controls and the README changelog.

Verified in a Studio playtest, locked on to the training dummy:
- D gives RollRight, moving 12 studs right while facing the target (facing dot at least 0.99).
- S gives RollBack, moving 13.6 back.
- A gives RollLeft, moving 12.4 left while facing the target.
- Unlocked rolls are unchanged.
- Posed frames confirm left tips left and back rolls backward.
- No console errors.
