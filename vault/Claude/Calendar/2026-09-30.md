## Sword feel pass

Goal: fix the "stiff" sword, following [[Combat and movement research (2026-09)]] (top 3 ranked changes).

Changed in the live place:
- `ReplicatedStorage.Combat.Animations.Specs`
  - New `stepFeet()` helper.
  - Light1, Light2 and Light3 rewritten with 10 keys each: anticipation with the weight back, then the hips fire first, then torso, arm and blade. The blade crosses the front on a Linear key, the cut ends on Out-eased keys with an overshoot, then a slow settle to guard. The lead foot steps in on the lunge.
  - Light2 and Light3 now start from the previous swing's end pose, so combos flow.
  - New markers RollOpen and MoveOpen.
  - Lengths are now 1.15 / 1.15 / 1.4 s (settle tail). Gameplay lengths are unchanged at 0.72 / 0.72 / 0.95 s.
  - The KnightSlash and KnightDelayedChop poses are untouched.
- `AnimationLibrary`: new `ActionLength(name)` (MoveOpen or full length) and `RollOpenTime(name)` (RollOpen, else CancelOpen).
- `WeaponBehaviors.BaseWeapon`
  - `Ended` fires at MoveOpen. The track keeps playing as a settle tail.
  - New `StopSettle` / `HasSettle`. The tail is stopped by a new attack, CancelAttack, blocking and charging.
- `WeaponController` (client)
  - Staggered cancels: the next light at CancelOpen, a roll at RollOpen.
  - A buffered input waits for its own window, and the newest press replaces an older one.
  - Moving during the settle tail fades it out.
- `CombatService` (server): rolls check RollOpen, and action length = ActionLength.
- Rebuilt all AnimSaves with AnimationBuilder.
- Updated `Docs.Combat` (state machine and animations sections) and the `Docs.README` changelog.

Timing that changed for players: a roll out of Light1/Light2 now opens at 0.58 s instead of 0.50 s. Out of Light3 it opens at 0.72 s instead of 0.64 s. Everything else keeps the old timing. These numbers are tuning judgments, not sourced.

Verified in a Studio playtest:
- No errors, and no server validation rejections through a full 3-hit combo.
- Hips lead the torso: at the hips-fire key the hips are at +6° while the waist is still at -23°.
- The lead foot steps about 1 stud and stays planted while the root lunges 0.87 studs. The rear foot slides 0.1 studs during Light1/2 and about 0.4 during Light3.
- The state returns to Idle at MoveOpen (0.73 s) while the animation keeps settling for about 0.5 s more.
- The settle tail fades when a new attack starts and when the player moves.

Git: included in Henry's commit 1d58e7f.

## Momentum and deflect research (no code changes)

Henry's feedback after the feel pass: attacking still kills all momentum, left click is still stiff, and deflect needs better effects and sound (anime style, modern Roblox, not defaults).

Ran 3 research passes (Sekiro feel, momentum/input, modern Roblox VFX/SFX) and saved the results to [[Attack momentum and deflect feel research (2026-09-30)]].

Root causes found in our code:
- The attack speed multiplier drops to 0.15 instantly, and any combat state cancels sprint.
- The light attack fires on mouse release.
- The lunge replaces velocity and is small (about 1 stud).
- The parry stagger is 1.6 s, and the parry uses placeholder sound and sparks.

## Momentum and deflect pass

Implemented the plan from [[Attack momentum and deflect feel research (2026-09-30)]], items 1 to 7. Heavy stays on hold-left-click; Henry didn't pick a separate key, so the press-then-hold approach was used. All tuning numbers are judgment calls, not sourced.

Changed in the live place:
- `CombatConfig`
  - HeavyHoldThreshold comment updated.
  - Added WindupCancelTime 0.15.
  - LungeSpeed 12→20 and LungeTime 0.12→0.15, so a Lunge 1 swing now travels about 2 studs.
  - Added LungeAssistRange 8, LungeAssistAngle 60 and LungeStopDistance 3.2.
  - Added MomentumCarryTime 0.18 and SprintAttackLungeMultiplier 1.8.
  - MoveSpeedMultipliers.Attacking 0.15→0.3.
  - Replaced ParryStaggerTime 1.6 with ParryRecoilTime 0.45, ParryPoiseMultiplier 1.0 and PerfectParryWindow 0.08.
  - HitStop adds Parry 0.1 and PerfectParry 0.14. Shake adds PerfectParry 0.7. New Punch table.
- `StaminaService:Refund` (new).
- `PoiseService`: `Stagger(..., keepPoise)` and a new `DrainPoise` (drains poise without a flinch).
- `DamageService` parry path
  - Drains the attacker's poise. An empty bar means a full stagger; otherwise a 0.45 s "ParryRecoil".
  - Perfect tier when the block was raised 0.08 s or less before the hit.
  - `fireEffect` now also sends the attacker model.
- `SparringKnightService`: a parried knight plays Flinch instead of the long Stagger.
- `CombatService`
  - `ChargeStart(fromLight)` turns the early light into a heavy and refunds its stamina.
  - Block and roll can cancel a light's first WindupCancelTime.
  - Block uses canAct "Block".
- `BaseWeapon`: `ReportHit` calls `weapon.HitLanded`.
- `WeaponController` (client)
  - Swing on press; a hold turns it into a heavy.
  - `carryMomentum()` keeps velocity into the swing.
  - `lunge()` with target assist, stop distance and a stop on contact.
  - Sprint attack.
  - Wind-up cancel into block or roll; block can also cancel after CancelOpen.
  - Parry reactions: a held deflect pose with camera shake and punch for the parrier, and a short recoil for the parried attacker.
- `Effects`
  - Parry vs block are now clearly different.
  - New parry burst with star flash and ForceField shockwave.
  - Layered clang whose pitch rises on consecutive parries.
  - Highlight flash and freeze on the attacker.
  - ImpactFrame on perfect parries.
  - Random pitch on hits and blocks.
  - New helpers `FlashModel`, `FreezeModel` and `ImpactFrame`.
- `Combat.Assets.Sounds`
  - Parry → Pro Sound Effects 9125669267.
  - New ParryRing → 9125672551. Both are public SFX library sounds.
  - Block made duller: volume 0.6, speed 0.6. The old Parry SoundId is saved in the attribute PreviousSoundId.
- Updated `Docs.Combat` (controls, state machine, defense, effects, placeholders) and the `Docs.README` changelog.

Verified in a Studio playtest:
- No client or server errors and no validation rejections.
- Tap: Light1 starts 30 ms after mouse down, before release (it used to wait for release).
- Hold: Light1 converts to a heavy charge about 0.23 s after press, before its 0.26 s hit, and stamina is refunded (88→100). The heavy fires on release.
- Momentum: walking at 16 studs/s into an attack, speed eases 15.5→13.9→7.5→4.8 instead of snapping to 2.4. The lunge peaks around 17 to 19 studs/s. The heavy lunge peaks at 35 studs/s.
- Parry loop against the Sparring Knight, driven by a server test hook that raised block 0.05 s before each knight hit:
  - A parry drains knight poise 60→38 with a 0.47 s Flinch recoil.
  - The next parries break posture into a full 1.1 s Stagger, and poise refills.
  - Parry and impact-frame effects ran on the client with no errors.

Henry's playtest feedback (2026-09-30): the parry looks really solid and momentum no longer gets stuck, but parry was too hard to hit (fixed 2026-10-01).

Not verified: wind-up cancel and roll-buffer timing (the MCP input tool adds 100 to 300 ms between actions), and the sprint attack.
