## Echoes weapon types

There are six types, and you can only swap between quests in town [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-demo-guide]:
- **Sword (+ optional shield):** balanced. Dropping the shield gives faster, riskier moves.
- **Two-Handed Axe:** slow, AoE, long reach, no shield [https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/sword-art-online/news/echoes-of-aincrad-everything-you-need-know].
- **Dagger:** fast, frequent hits, short range, lower damage, can be thrown, no shield.
- **Rapier:** long reach, chains attacks, can counter, shield allowed.
- **Two-Handed Sword:** charged heavy hits, and its dodge becomes a roll. No shield.
- **Mace:** shield allowed. It breaks defenses to stagger or knock enemies down.

All six have Light, Heavy and Crush attacks. Ver 1.0.6 sped up Mace, 2H Sword and 2H Axe basics and raised their damage [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-6-distribution-announcement]. Crush is a held heavy that damages guards [https://thegameswiki.com/echoes-of-aincrad/wiki/combat-system].

## Echoes sword skill system

- **SP:** Sword Skills spend SP, which is separate from Stamina. Stamina pays for attacks, dodges and shield guards [https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/sword-art-online/news/echoes-of-aincrad-gameplay-mechanics-explained].
- **Slots and unlocks:** up to 3 equipped. More Weapon Exp unlocks more skills [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-demo-guide].
- **Per-weapon SP gain from normal and heavy hits:** Ver 1.0.5 lowered it for fast many-hit weapons (Sword, Rapier, Dagger, Mace) and raised it for slow ones (2H Sword, 2H Axe) [https://bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-5-distribution-announcement].
- **Cooldowns and charging:** each skill has its own cooldown and SP cost. 2H skills charge, and some skills have Super Armor (same 1.0.5 source).
- **Proficiency:** caps at 50. The last skill unlocks at proficiency 10 for every weapon. After that, each level gives a skill point, and each level costs a fixed 1,020 exp. A town quest unlocks skill upgrades [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/629909-echoes-of-aincrad/81172218]. Skills max at level 5, with about 10 per weapon [https://www.echoesofaincrad.org/sword-skills/] (fan site).
- **Chaining:** there is no Skill Connect. Basics and skills combine freely within SP and Stamina limits [https://thegameswiki.com/echoes-of-aincrad/wiki/sword-skills] (fan wiki).
- **Slash counters:**
  - Parry Slash comes from a perfect parry, and parrying needs a shield.
  - Dodge Slash comes from a perfect dodge.
  - Reversal Slash comes from hitting an enemy while a blue ring shows during its wind-up.
  - All three open a counter with your partner [https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2026/06/echoes-of-aincrad-hands-on-preview-that-time-i-got-transported-into-a-video-game-again/].
  - The Day 1 patch changed Slash damage and lengthened step i-frames [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-day-one-patch].
- **Partner:** one AI partner per quest [https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/sword-art-online/news/echoes-of-aincrad-everything-you-need-know].
  - Switch Mode: the partner attacks your target, and you take turns.
  - Free Mode: the partner attacks other enemies.
  - Partner Concentration drains in Free Mode and refills in Switch Mode [https://thegameswiki.com/echoes-of-aincrad/wiki/combat-system].
  - Combination Skills unlock after you deal enough damage [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-demo-guide].
- **Break:** bosses can have a Break Marker that drives phase changes, and flying enemies can lose their wings [https://thegameswiki.com/echoes-of-aincrad/wiki/combat-system] (fan wiki). The 1.0.6 patch mentions a knockdown ("down") meter [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-6-distribution-announcement].
- **Not found:** an Echoes burst or finisher system. No source showed one.

## Reception

- **Critics:** OpenCritic top critic average is about 63 [https://opencritic.com/game/20207/echoes-of-aincrad/reviews]. Steam is Mixed at 51% across 2,361 reviews [https://store.steampowered.com/app/2244210/].
- **Praise:**
  - Combat is called "the best part of the package." The six weapons feel distinct rather than reskins, and bosses are the highlight [https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/echoes-of-aincrad-review/].
  - TheSixthAxis liked its "methodical flow" [https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/07/13/echoes-of-aincrad-review/].
  - Players on Steam called it "highly polished" with flashy skill animations [https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/2244210?json=1&filter=all&language=english].
- **Criticism:**
  - Depth plateaus by the midpoint, and swapping weapons is tedious [https://butwhytho.net/2026/07/echoes-of-aincrad-review-bandai-namco/].
  - Enemies recycle constantly [https://noobfeed.com/reviews/echoes-of-aincrad-review].
  - The preview build was "ridiculously easy" because stamina was lenient and skills recharged fast [https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2026/06/echoes-of-aincrad-hands-on-preview-that-time-i-got-transported-into-a-video-game-again/].
  - It "lacks the visual impact" the RPG Site previewer hoped for [https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/20538-echoes-of-aincrad-pc-hands-on-preview-port-steam-deck-rog-ally].
  - Steam players say dodging feels stiff, hitboxes hit enemies behind you, and harder modes just inflate damage [https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/2244210?json=1&filter=all&language=english].
  - On GameFAQs, a player said the 3 slots are "mostly stand alone moves," compared with older games' Skill Connect and skill buffs [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/629909-echoes-of-aincrad/81172733]. Others found normal mode "quite easy" and wanted on-hit feedback [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/629909-echoes-of-aincrad/81159352].

## Lessons for Sword Bound (ranked)

1. **Make skills the punish, not the opener.** Reviewers found skills strongest inside parry and dodge windows [https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/echoes-of-aincrad-review/]. Give skills bonus damage on posture-broken or riposte states (inferred, UNSOURCED for the bonus itself).
2. **Normalize SP gain per weapon.** Echoes had to cut SP gain for fast many-hit weapons [https://bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-5-distribution-announcement]. Because Sword Bound refills SP from basic hits, award SP per damage or per second of swing, not per hit (inferred).
3. **Make the Skill Connect cue loud and readable.** Last Recollection uses a light-blue flash for success and a black light for failure, with a 3-connect base [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/sword-art-online-last-recollection-101-guide], [https://steamcommunity.com/app/1566880/discussions/0/3879345465238799262]. Hollow Realization players chain on a "blue flash" [https://steamcommunity.com/app/607890/discussions/0/3277925755451737025].
4. **Avoid the mastery grind.** Hollow Realization needed 100, 1,000 and 10,000 uses for skill stars [https://steamcommunity.com/app/607890/discussions/0/1482109512300919143]. Unlocking dual wield took about 28 hours [https://steamcommunity.com/app/607890/discussions/0/3277925755451737025]. Echoes' fixed 1,020 exp per level with all skills by proficiency 10 was praised [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/629909-echoes-of-aincrad/81172218].
5. **Do not make difficulty out of HP and damage inflation.** Echoes patches kept raising boss HP [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-6-distribution-announcement], and players objected [https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/2244210?json=1&filter=all&language=english]. Lycoris players called its bosses "health tank[s]" [https://steamcommunity.com/app/1009290/discussions/0/4336358006704352721].
6. **Give each weapon one defining mechanic.** Echoes used shield parry, throw, charge plus roll, counter, break, and AoE [https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-demo-guide]. Make swapping weapons painless [https://butwhytho.net/2026/07/echoes-of-aincrad-review-bandai-namco/].
7. **Give skills effects beyond damage.** Players wanted buffs and riders, not standalone moves [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/629909-echoes-of-aincrad/81172733].
8. **Define Super Armor and interrupt priority explicitly.** Echoes patched multi-hit damage taken during Super Armor [https://bandainamcoent.com/news/echoes-of-aincrad-update-file-ver-1-0-5-distribution-announcement], and no priority ranking has been published [https://thegameswiki.com/echoes-of-aincrad/wiki/parry-slash-dodge-slash-and-reversal-slash] (fan wiki).
